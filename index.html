Android apps for older people and the families who look after them.

About

AshBashApps Limited is a small UK software company. It began with one phone, built for a 96-year-old mother who needed to make and take calls with a single tap however tired or confused she was, and grew into an app that other families could set up for the person they look after.

What we make

Tom's Phone

Their whole phone: a few names, and SOS.

Tom's Phone turns an ordinary Android phone into a dedicated phone for an older or vulnerable person, set up and looked after by you. They see one screen: their name, a few large buttons with the names of the people they call, and an SOS button. The ring says their name; a missed call from you can be repeated out loud until a call between you connects; SOS calls the emergency services after a deliberate confirmation, and starts in test; their medical information is there for whoever comes to help. No account, no analytics, and names, numbers and medical information stay on the phone. Free for 30 days, then £20 once or £1 a month, in the UK.
