AshBashApps

Android apps for older people and the families who look after them.

About

AshBashApps Limited is a small UK software company. It began with one phone, built for a 96-year-old mother who needed to make and take calls with a single tap however tired or confused she was, and grew into an app that other families and carers could set up for the person they look after.

What we make

Tom's Phone

A dedicated phone for an older or vulnerable person. Big one-tap call buttons, a spoken ring that names who is calling, missed-call reminders, an SOS button for the emergency services, and a medical card for responders. A family member or carer sets it up and looks after it; the person using it sees only their home screen. Works without an account and keeps its data on the phone.
